1. Data Collection

The Game is a paid, offline single-player title. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information, including but not limited to: name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, IP address, or advertising identifiers (AAID).

Your game progress and settings are stored only in your device's app-specific storage. No save data is synced to the cloud or any server. Uninstalling the Game will permanently delete all save data.

The Game does not request any sensitive Android permissions beyond those automatically granted by the system.

The Game is distributed as a one-time paid purchase through Google Play. All payment processing is handled by Google Play; the Game's developer does not have access to your payment information. Please refer to Google Play's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13, and does not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date above will be revised accordingly.
